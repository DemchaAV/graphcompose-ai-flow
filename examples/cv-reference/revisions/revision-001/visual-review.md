## Known Differences

- Contact/social icons are placeholders instead of Iconify assets.
- The expertise check badge is simplified.
- Skill bars are text approximations rather than measured vector rules.
- Font tracking and exact body typography are approximate.
- No visual-diff score is available yet for the two-page reference.

## Recommendation

Use this as the baseline draft. The next revision should focus on icons,
the expertise badge, and skill bars before chasing smaller spacing
differences.
